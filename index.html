  • Our streaming concept focuses on beginner-friendly digital art tutorials, calm pacing, clear guidance, and encouraging community connection. We aim to make art learning feel welcoming, practical, and easy to follow for viewers who want to improve their drawing skills.

  • Audience Analysis: Our target audience are viewers aged 10-34+ female, any nationality, marital status, or employment status who are interested in learning digital art, new techniques, exploring art styles, and improving their skills. They can be beginner or experienced artists who enjoy art and would like to branch out their portfolio/skills by trying out digital art. Viewer Needs: Our viewers need clear explanations, helpful digital art demonstrations, and good audio and visual quality. They also need opportunities to ask questions and interact during the livestream. Our content should be welcoming and easy to understand for different skill levels. Characteristics of a Good Stream: Clear Audio Quality - Good sound quality is more important than a high resolution video. Constant Narration - Narrating actions and thinking out loud keeps new visitors from leaving. Community Interaction - A safe and welcoming environment, encouraging viewers to participate in the chat, and responding to audience messages. Consistent Schedule - Broadcasting at predictable days and times helps regular viewers plan to attend. Authenticity and Personality - Viewers often stay for the entertainer’s unique humor, positive attitude and genuine vibe rather than just high-level skill.

The platform we will use for our broadcasting software will be YouTube.The reason why we picked Youtube as our broadcast software is because it has livestreaming built into the main app/website. It’s easier to access and understand rather than other broadcasting platforms such as Twitch. The tools we will be using for our streams: Phone/Computer - for streaming and managing the live stream. tablet or phone - for creating and showing the digital art. Drawing pad and pen - for drawing digitally and showing our art techniques. Microphone/headset - to communicate clearly with the viewers. Stable internet - to keep the Livestream smooth.

How have your ideas developed? Our ideas of doing an Art Livestream developed due to some of our members’ background with creating art, specifically digitally. In our Determining The Right Content for Target Audience Worksheet, from a survey done by CELSYS, most traditional artists would like to try out digital art, and trying out digital art out to a traditional artist means trying something new. And we would like to help with improving their art skills digitally, and teach them the basics, features, etc. of IbisPaint specifically. Why did you pick Art as your content decision? We arrived at the decision that Art is going to be our content, as our members have a background in creating art, and strive to help others with their digital art journey by showing them the basics, features, pros and cons of doing digital art, the equipment needed, etc.
